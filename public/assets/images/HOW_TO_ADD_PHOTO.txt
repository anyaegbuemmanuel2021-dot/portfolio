HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTO
==============================

1. Get a professional photo (square or portrait format works best)
2. Save it as:  profile.jpg  (or profile.png)
3. Place it in this folder:  assets/images/

4. Then in index.html, find this comment:
   <!-- ── SWAP THIS BLOCK WITH YOUR PHOTO ── -->
   
   Replace the entire <div class="profile-photo__placeholder"> block with:
   <img src="assets/images/profile.jpg" alt="Anyaegbu Emmanuel Ikenna" />

5. Do the same on about.html inside the <div class="about-photo-frame">

RECOMMENDED PHOTO SPECS:
  - Minimum: 600 × 800 px
  - Ideal:    1000 × 1300 px
  - Format:   JPG or WebP
  - Lighting: Clean background, good lighting
  - Style:    Professional but relaxed

You can use a free background remover at: remove.bg
