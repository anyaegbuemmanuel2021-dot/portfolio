Ideas, guides and development notes

Explore practical writing about planning and building websites, improving user experience, making pages work across devices and creating a stronger foundation for search engines. New articles are published through the portfolio content system.

The topics include semantic HTML, CSS layout, JavaScript interactions, accessibility, website structure, page speed, metadata, internal linking and the technical foundations that help search engines understand a site. Where a subject involves a project or implementation, the aim is to explain the decisions and trade-offs rather than present a list of buzzwords.

These notes are intended for business owners, students and developers who want practical explanations of how websites are planned, built, improved and maintained. Use the search and category filters below to find a published article, or get in touch to discuss a website or digital project.
