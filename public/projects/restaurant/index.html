Our Story

Cooking With Love Since 2015

What started as Mama Nneka's home kitchen in Lagos has grown into one of the most beloved Nigerian restaurants in the city. We use only the freshest local ingredients, sourced daily from Bodija Market.

Every dish is a tribute to the rich culinary heritage of Nigeria — from the smoky jollof to the bold flavours of our soups. We believe good food is the language of home.
