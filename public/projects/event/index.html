How We Met

Chidi was speaking at a tech panel. Amaka was in the front row, skeptical about every point he made. After the session, she walked up to challenge him — and he was completely disarmed by her intelligence.

He asked for her number "to continue the debate." Three years later, he's still losing arguments — and loving every minute of it.
